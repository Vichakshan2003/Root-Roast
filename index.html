Our Story

Where heritage ingredients meet modern snacking

Root & Roast began with a simple idea: Indian pantry ingredients deserve a fresher, more elevated place in everyday snacking. We loved the light texture and cultural depth of Phool Makhana, and we saw the same opportunity in millet-based foods — nourishing, versatile, and deeply rooted in tradition.

So we built a brand that celebrates both. One side of the collection highlights roasted lotus seeds in bold and classic flavors. The other brings forward millet snacks like Ragi Thattai, Chivda, Cereal, Crackers, and Puffs for a pantry that feels wholesome, flavorful, and intentional.
